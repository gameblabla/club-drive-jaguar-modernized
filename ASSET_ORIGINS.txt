Club Drive — asset origins

This repository is a reconstruction, not a historically complete or officially
published Atari source tree. Some resources were preserved in surviving 3D Studio
and assembly-era source files; other portions were recovered from the retail
cartridge and converted into editable authoring formats.

The folders under src/assets are named for what they contain, not how they
were obtained. Editable PNG, WAV, MIDI, ASC and OBJ inputs are separate from
Jaguar-native compressed/packed resources under src/assets/native. The native
references remain necessary for byte-for-byte reproduction of the retail ROM.

The generated 3D/BSP data and the editable score representations are not
represented as original 1990s release formats unless supported by the inputs.
